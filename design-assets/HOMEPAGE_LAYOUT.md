# PetStore1 Homepage Layout Specification

## Section Order & Structure

### 1. Announcement Bar (Optional)
- Location: Top of page, above header
- Content: "Free delivery on orders over €39 | Irish-owned & operated"
- Style: Fresh Green background, white text
- Height: 40px

### 2. Header / Navigation
- Logo: Left side, "Paws & Friends" text logo or custom image
- Mega Menu Navigation: Dogs, Cats, Fish & Aquatics, Small Animals, Birds, Reptiles, Deals, Subscribe & Save
- Right side icons: Search (with autocomplete), Account, Cart with item count badge
- Mobile: Hamburger menu, centered logo

### 3. Hero Banner
**Dimensions:**
- Desktop: 1600x600px full-width
- Tablet: 100% width x 400px
- Mobile: 100% width x 300px

**Layout:**
- Full-bleed background image (happy pet + owner, outdoor Irish setting)
- Dark gradient overlay (bottom 50% gradient to transparent)
- Left-aligned text content
- Headline: "Everything your pet needs" (H1, white, 48px desktop / 32px mobile)
- Subheadline: "Free delivery on orders over €39" (20px, white with 90% opacity)
- CTA Button: "Shop Now" - Fresh Green (#2E8B57), white text, rounded corners (8px)
- Button hover: Darker green (#247348)

**Mobile Layout:**
- Image stretches full-width
- Text overlay centered
- CTA button full-width

### 4. Shop by Pet Tile Grid
**Layout:**
- Desktop: 3 columns x 2 rows, equal spacing
- Tablet: 3 columns x 2 rows, smaller tiles
- Mobile: 2 columns x 3 rows

**6 Pet Categories:**
1. Dogs
2. Cats
3. Fish & Aquatics
4. Small Animals
5. Birds
6. Reptiles

**Tile Design:**
- Image: 600x400px, pet portrait with friendly expression
- Overlay: Subtle gradient at bottom
- Pet name: White text, centered, overlaid on gradient
- "Shop Now" link: Appears on hover (desktop)
- Card style: White background, 12px border-radius, subtle shadow
- Hover state: Lift up 4px, deeper shadow

**Image Recommendations:**
- Dogs: Happy golden retriever outdoors
- Cats: Curious tabby cat looking at camera
- Fish & Aquatics: Colorful tropical fish in aquarium
- Small Animals: Fluffy rabbit in garden
- Birds: Colorful parrot/perched bird
- Reptiles: Friendly gecko on leaf

### 5. Deal of the Day Strip
**Location:** Below Shop by Pet, full-width
**Background:** Gradient from Coral (#FF7675) to lighter coral
**Layout:**
- "DEAL OF THE DAY" label (bold, white, 14px)
- Product thumbnail (80x80px, rounded)
- Product name (white, truncate if needed)
- Original price (white, strikethrough, smaller)
- Deal price (white, bold, larger)
- "Ends in [timer]" text (white, 90% opacity)
- "Shop Deals" CTA link (white, underlined)

### 6. Bestsellers Carousel
**Section Title:** "Bestsellers" (H2, Charcoal)
**Background:** Warm Cream (#FDF6E3)
**Layout:**
- Horizontal scrolling carousel
- Desktop: 4 products visible, scroll arrows on sides
- Tablet: 3 products visible
- Mobile: 2 products visible, swipe to scroll

**Product Card (inside carousel):**
- Image: 300x300px, hover zoom effect
- Star rating: 5 stars (Coral color), review count below
- Product name: Charcoal, 16px, 2 lines max
- Price: Fresh Green (#2E8B57), bold
- Quick Add button: Small "Add to Cart" button below price

### 7. Trust Badges Section
**Background:** White
**Layout:** 3 badges in a row, centered, equal spacing

**3 Trust Badges:**
1. **Irish-Owned**
   - Icon: Map of Ireland or flag icon (64x64px)
   - Icon background: Sky Blue (#74B9FF)
   - Text: "Irish-Owned & Operated"
   - Subtext: "Supporting local communities"

2. **Fast Dispatch**
   - Icon: Truck or package icon
   - Icon background: Fresh Green (#2E8B57)
   - Text: "Fast Dispatch"
   - Subtext: "Same day if ordered before 2pm"

3. **14-Day Returns**
   - Icon: Return/refresh icon
   - Icon background: Soft Teal (#3CB8A8)
   - Text: "14-Day Returns"
   - Subtext: "No questions asked"

**Mobile:** Stack vertically, full-width badges

### 8. Newsletter Signup
**Background:** Warm Sand (#F5E6D3)
**Layout:** Centered content, max-width 600px

**Content:**
- Headline: "Get 10% Off Your First Order" (H2)
- Subtext: "Subscribe to our newsletter for exclusive deals and pet care tips"
- Email input field: White background, rounded, placeholder "Enter your email"
- CTA Button: "Get 10% Off" - Fresh Green background, white text
- Privacy note: Small text below, "We respect your privacy. Unsubscribe anytime."

**Mobile:** Full-width input and button, stacked

### 9. Footer
**Background:** Charcoal (#2D3436)
**Columns:**
1. Shop (Dogs, Cats, Fish, Small Animals, Birds, Reptiles, Deals)
2. Customer Service (Contact Us, Shipping Info, Returns, FAQ)
3. About (Our Story, Blog, Careers)
4. Contact (hello@pawsandfriends.ie, social icons)
